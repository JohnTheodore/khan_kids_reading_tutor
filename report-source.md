# Khan Academy Kids school dashboard: research source

Audience: Parent evaluating Khan Academy Kids for a reading-only lesson plan  
Date: 2026-09-08  
Scope: Current U.S. Khan Academy Kids home, free Class Account, and paid school/district dashboard offerings.

## Direct answer

The official web dashboard is https://dashboard.khanacademykids.org/. A parent can convert an existing home Parent Account to a free Class Account in the Khan Kids mobile app, and the child's existing progress carries over. That conversion unlocks Teacher Tools in the mobile app, but does not grant access to the web dashboard. Khan states that web-dashboard access is provisioned only to school or district partners under a signed institutional agreement and cannot be purchased by an individual teacher or homeschool user.

## Findings

### Portal and access

- Official portal: https://dashboard.khanacademykids.org/
- Khan's activation guide says provisioned teachers can sign in there through Clever or with their Khan Kids email and password.
- The public Teacher Dashboard help page says users may sign in with their Khan Kids credentials only "once access is granted."
- Khan lists the school/district package at $5 per student. The public materials do not clearly specify the billing period.
- The offering is designed for groups of 200 or more. Smaller schools are told to contact the partnership team, but individual teachers and homeschool users cannot purchase access.

### Free Parent-to-Class conversion

- In the app: For Parents / Grown-Ups section -> account dropdown -> Teacher: Convert to a Class Account.
- Khan says existing child profiles and progress carry over.
- The resulting Class Account includes free in-app Teacher Tools: the full Pre-K through grade 2 lesson library, standards search, assignments, lesson previews, and progress reports.
- Lessons expose Basic, Main, Practice 1, and Practice 2 variants. Khan recommends Main first, Basic for extra support, then Practice variants after mastery.
- Teacher settings can hide Create and Videos and can require assignments to be completed first.
- Khan's public documentation does not describe a self-service route for converting a Class Account back to a Parent Account.

### Paid web dashboard

- Search lessons by grade, skill, or keyword.
- Preview and fast-forward lesson content.
- Assign lessons and manage assignments.
- View usage, completion, assignment, and skill-progress reports.
- Access is enabled through a school/district partnership rather than merely by changing the account type.

### Implication for this project

The free Class Account is likely sufficient for building a reading-only sequence through Android automation. It exposes substantially better curriculum structure than the normal child-facing library. The paid browser dashboard would make crawling easier, but there is no documented legitimate individual/home purchase path.

## Limitations

- Khan does not publish a general self-service checkout or individual subscription for the web dashboard.
- Public sources do not specify whether an existing converted home account can later be attached to an institutional partnership; Khan would need to confirm this.
- Public documentation does not explain how to reverse a Parent-to-Class conversion.
