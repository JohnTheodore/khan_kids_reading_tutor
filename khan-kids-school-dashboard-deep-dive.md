# Khan Kids school dashboard: can a parent get access?

*Verified September 8, 2026 from Khan Academy's current documentation.*

## Bottom line

The web portal is **https://dashboard.khanacademykids.org/**.

You can convert your existing Parent Account to a free Class Account, and Khan says your child's progress will carry over. However, this gives you Teacher Tools **inside the mobile app only**. It does **not** unlock the website.

Khan explicitly says the web Teacher Dashboard:

- is available only to school or district partners;
- cannot be purchased by an individual teacher or homeschool user; and
- requires a signed institutional agreement and account provisioning.

## What you can do for free

In Khan Kids, open the grown-ups/parent section, use the account dropdown, and select **Teacher: Convert to a Class Account**. Khan's [conversion instructions](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042944391-FAQ-How-do-I-convert-my-account-to-a-Class-Account) say existing profiles and progress carry over.

The free Class Account is unusually relevant to a reading-only setup. Its in-app Teacher Tools provide:

- every Pre-K through grade 2 lesson in the teacher library;
- browsing by Letters, Reading, Books, and other domains;
- standards-based search;
- lesson previews and a fast-forward preview option;
- assignment scheduling and individual assignment;
- Basic, Main, Practice 1, and Practice 2 lesson variants; and
- assignment and progress reports.

Khan also provides teacher settings to hide the Create and Videos tabs and require students to complete assignments first. See Khan's [Teacher Tools overview](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041862972-All-about-Teacher-Tools-in-Khan-Academy-Kids), [assignment guide](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042194831-Module-3-Assigning-lessons), and [student-access settings](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/17031195532059--NEW-Manage-student-access-to-areas-of-the-app).

## What the paid web dashboard adds

The [web Teacher Dashboard](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14738508196379--NEW-Teacher-Dashboard-on-the-web) supports searching by grade, skill, or keyword; previewing and fast-forwarding lessons; assigning lessons; and viewing assignment, usage, completion, and skill-progress reports. Khan's official [activation guide](https://sites.google.com/khanacademy.org/khanacademykids/activating-your-account) directs provisioned teachers to **https://dashboard.khanacademykids.org/** and offers Clever or Khan Kids email/password sign-in.

The important phrase is **"once access is granted."** Having valid Khan Kids credentials or converting to Class mode is not enough by itself.

## Can a family buy it?

Not currently. Khan says the listed package costs **$5 per student**, is designed for school/district groups of 200 or more, and cannot be purchased by individual teachers or homeschool users. Smaller actual schools can contact `khankidspartners@khanacademy.org`, but Khan does not promise acceptance. See Khan's [access policy](https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14738508196379--NEW-Teacher-Dashboard-on-the-web) and [individual/homeschool qualification page](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids/schools-districts/partnership-inquiry-thank-you-1).

Publicly funded Pre-K programs may qualify for Khan's announced free pilot for the 2026–27 and 2027–28 school years through the [school partnership inquiry](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids/schools-districts/partnership-inquiry).

## Recommendation

Convert to the free Class Account if you are comfortable changing the account's mode. It delivers the features most useful here—complete lesson visibility, previews, sequencing variants, assignments, and stronger restrictions—without needing the paid website. Then automate the teacher interface on Android.

One caution: Khan documents conversion *to* Class Account but does not document a self-service conversion back to Parent Account. If reversibility matters, ask Khan support first or test Class mode with a separate account before converting the account containing your child's progress.
